01 • Core Concepts

Let's Understand Motion

Before using equations, let's understand what the quantities mean.

📍

Distance

Distance is the total length of the path travelled by an object. It is a scalar quantity.

🧭

Displacement

Displacement is the distance moved in a particular direction from the starting point.

🏃

Speed

Speed tells us how quickly distance is covered.

speed = distance ÷ time
🚀

Velocity

Velocity is speed in a particular direction. It is a vector quantity.

⚡

Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

a = change in velocity ÷ time

04 • Graphs

Reading Motion Graphs

Graphs allow us to describe motion visually.

📏

Distance–Time Graph

The gradient of a distance–time graph gives the speed. A steeper line means greater speed.

📈

Velocity–Time Graph

The gradient gives acceleration. The area under the graph gives distance travelled.
